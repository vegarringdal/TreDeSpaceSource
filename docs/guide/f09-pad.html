App layout · F9

The Pad layout

Press F9 (or pick Pad on the App Layout ribbon; on a tablet, tap the Pad ribbon tab) to get the touch workspace. The Pad ribbon puts the things you would normally do with keys and mouse buttons on large buttons that are easy to hit with a finger. There is no panel on the right, so the 3D view gets as much room as possible.

  1. Pad ribbon — described group by group below.
  2. Hierarchy — the model tree. Tap a row to select that item and everything under it.
  3. 3D view — tap an item to select it, tap empty space to unselect everything.
  4. Console — minimised. Tap its tab to see messages.

Moving around with your fingers

  • One finger, drag — turns the view (orbits around the pivot, or looks around in Fly and Walk).
  • Two fingers, drag — moves the view sideways and up/down.
  • Two fingers, pinch — zooms in and out.
  • Tap — selects. A finger may wander a little during a tap; a short wobble does not turn the view.

Ribbon

The Pad ribbon

View

  1. Fly To (ALT 625) — flies the camera to the selection, so it fills the view.
  2. Focus Click (O) — makes the last point you tapped on the model the new pivot: from now on the view turns around that point. The camera stays where it is and turns to face it. (With a mouse: Alt + click.)
  3. Go To Click (G) — flies closer to the last point you tapped and makes it the pivot. (With a mouse: Space + click.)
  4. Focus Sel. (ALT 1029) — makes the middle of the selection the pivot. The camera stays where it is.
  5. Fit Visible (ALT 1219) — frames everything that is not hidden or clipped away. Hide, isolate or clip first to zoom onto one area.
  6. Solo (ALT 626) — closes every panel except the 3D view, for the biggest possible picture. Tap again to get the panels back.
  7. Hide (H) — hides the selected items.
  8. Reset All (ALT+R) — shows every hidden item and removes every colour and opacity change on the whole model.
  9. Clear Sel. (ESC) — unselects everything.

For more on hiding and colouring, see F4 Selection Color.

Tree and Nav

  1. Up (U) — selects the parent of the selected item: one level up in the model tree. Tap it again to go further up, for example from a single part to its pipe line, then to the whole system.
  2. Down (P) — goes back down again, one level at a time, towards the item you first selected.
  3. Orbit (ALT 627) — the view only turns around the pivot and moves sideways; nothing flies forward.
  4. Fly (ALT 628) — moving forward goes where you are looking, so looking down and moving forward takes you down. This is the default.
  5. Walk (ALT 629) — moving forward keeps you at the same height, like walking on a deck.

In Orbit, pushing the move joystick (or a movement key on a keyboard) switches to Fly by itself, or to Walk if Walk when movement keys are used is ticked under Settings → Navigation. Focus Click, Go To Click and zooming with a mouse wheel switch back to Orbit.

Controller

  1. Joystick (ALT 630) — shows or hides the two on-screen joysticks in the 3D view.
  2. Top — how far down from the top edge both joysticks sit, in percent of the view's height (default 50 %, the middle).
  3. Side — how far in from the side edge each joystick sits, in percent of the view's width (default 10 %). Both move together, the left one from the left edge and the right one from the right edge.

Move them to where your thumbs rest when you hold the tablet.

Step by step

Common tasks

Walk through the plant with the joysticks

  1. Tap Walk under Nav, so you stay at the same height.
  2. Tap Joystick. Two circles appear in the 3D view. 1 The left joystick moves you: push up to go forward, down to go back, left or right to step sideways. The further you push, the faster you go. 2 The right joystick turns your head: left and right to turn, up and down to look up and down.
  3. You can still drag with two fingers on the view to move sideways. Let go of a joystick and you stop at once.
  4. Tap Joystick again to hide them.

How fast you move is set under Settings → Navigation (Fly and Walk speeds).

Look at one item up close

  1. Tap the item in the 3D view, or its row in the Hierarchy.
  2. Tap Up once or twice if you want the whole assembly the item belongs to.
  3. Tap Fly To. The view frames the selection, and one-finger drags now turn around it.
  4. To look at a spot on it from closer, tap that spot, then Go To Click.
  5. Tap Fit Visible to get the whole model back in view.

Measure on a tablet

Measuring works by touch too: pick a tool on the Measurement ribbon, then tap points, or touch and hold for a precise crosshair. See Measuring on a tablet.
