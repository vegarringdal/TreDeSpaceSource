Getting started

Before you start

Opening a panel

Every tool lives in a panel. The quickest way to get the right panels on screen is an app layout (F1–F12). You can also open any single panel from the Panel tab of the ribbon (see F8 Panels) or with its keyboard shortcut. A panel opens as a tab next to the others on its side of the screen. Drag its tab to move it, drag the line between two panels to resize them, and double-click the tab to float the panel in its own window.

Tooltips and shortcuts

Hold the mouse over any button for a moment to see what it does. If the button has a keyboard shortcut, the tooltip shows it at the bottom. Shortcuts are written like this:

  • A plus sign means at the same time (simultaneous keys): CTRL+C — hold Ctrl and press C.
  • A space means one after the other (consecutive keys): G X — press G, let go, then press X.
  • A number is typed digit by digit: ALT 1021 — press and release Alt, then type 1 0 2 1.

You can change any shortcut under Settings → Shortcuts. The shortcuts in this guide are the defaults.

About the pictures

The screenshots use the Huldra platform model, an open dataset published by Equinor. It has one model per discipline (piping, safety, telecom and so on) loaded into a folder called Huldra. Your own models will have different names, but the steps are the same.

App layouts

Twelve workspaces, one key each

An app layout sets up the whole screen for one kind of work: which ribbon tab is open and which panels are shown, and where. Press F1–F12 to switch. Switching only rearranges the screen: your models, selection and colours stay as they are. Each card opens the guide for that layout.

F1 Home Load models, camera, draw modes and screenshots. Settings on the right. F2 Clip Plane Cut the model with a plane to look inside. F3 Clip Box Show only what is inside a box, plus extra clip shapes. F4 Selection Color Colour, ghost, hide and isolate the selection. Set Color on the right. F5 Transform Move, rotate and scale selected items. F6 Measurements Measure distances and angles, with the Measurement List on the right. F7 External Open the external apps your organisation has configured. F8 Panels Show or hide any panel. Settings → Layouts on the right. F9 Pad Big buttons and a joystick for tablets and touch screens. F10 Viewpoint Create and present saved views with their own labels, colours and measurements. F11 SQL Editor Query the model data with SQL and colour the results. F12 Assets Import, load, export and manage models and databases.

F1–F9 each open one ribbon tab, plus the panel that goes with it where there is one. F10–F12 are bigger workspaces built around a group of panels; they use the Home ribbon.

Ribbon

The App Layout ribbon

The last ribbon tab, App Layout, lists every layout as a button, and is where you save your own.

  1. Preset layouts (F1–F12) — click one to switch to it.
  2. Your layouts (ALT+F1–ALT+F12, Config01–12) — empty until you save into them.
  3. Save — stores the current screen arrangement in the selected slot (the highlighted button).
  4. Linked Ribbon — the ribbon tab that opens when the selected layout is applied.
  5. Ribbon Open / Ribbon Closed — whether the ribbon is shown or folded away for that layout.

Save your own layout

  1. Arrange the screen the way you want: open panels from the Panel ribbon, drag their tabs to where you want them and resize them.
  2. Open the App Layout ribbon and click an empty slot, for example Config01 2. It is now the selected slot.
  3. Choose the Linked Ribbon 4 that should open with it, and whether the ribbon starts open or closed 5.
  4. Press Save 3. From now on ALT+F1 brings this arrangement back.

You can also save over a preset (select F1–F12 and press Save). The original can always be restored, see below.

Rename or reset a layout

Names are changed under Settings → Layouts (the F8 layout opens it). Type a new name 1. The reset button 2 puts a slot back to its original name and arrangement; it is only active for slots you have saved into. Reset layouts, at the bottom of the list, resets all 24 slots at once.
