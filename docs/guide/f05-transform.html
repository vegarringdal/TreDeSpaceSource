App layout · F5

The Transform layout

Press F5 (or pick Transform on the App Layout ribbon) to switch to the transform workspace. Everything on its ribbon works on the current selection: select items in the tree or the 3D view, then move, turn or resize them with the buttons, or drag them with the handles in the 3D view. The model files themselves are never changed; see what is kept.

  1. Transform ribbon — described group by group below.
  2. Hierarchy — the model tree. Click a row to select that item and everything under it.
  3. 3D view — click an item to select it; the handles appear on the selection.
  4. Console — minimised. Click its tab to see a line for each mode you switch on or off.

Ribbon

The Transform ribbon

Most buttons are greyed out until something is selected. Every move, turn, resize and reset can be undone with Undo (ALT 101). This undo is separate from the colour Undo on the Selection Color ribbon.

On/Off, Pivot and Options

  1. Move (ALT 110) — shows arrows on the selection in the 3D view. Drag an arrow to move the selection along it.
  2. Rotate (ALT 111) — shows three rings. Drag a ring to turn the selection around it. Hold Shift to turn in 15° steps.
  3. Scale (ALT 112) — shows a square handle on each arrow. Drag one to stretch or squeeze the selection in that direction only.
  4. Set Pivot (ALT 120) — places your own pivot: the point that Rotate and Scale turn and resize around. Without one, they use the middle of the selection. See Use a pivot. Once a pivot is set, the button reads Adjust.
  5. Reset (ALT 121) — forgets your pivot and goes back to the middle of the selection.
  6. Auto Disable (ALT 1226, on at the start) — when you leave this ribbon (another ribbon tab, or another layout), the handles, pivot placing and Move To Click are switched off, so a later click in the 3D view does not move anything by accident. A locked pivot is kept. The app remembers this choice.

Press the lit Move, Rotate or Scale button again to hide the handles. The handles always point along the model's X (red), Y (green) and Z (blue) directions, even after you have turned the item. A drag counts as one step for Undo.

Placing the pivot

  1. Lock — keeps the pivot where it is now.
  2. Cancel — drops the pivot and goes back to the middle of the selection.
  3. Item Pivot (ALT 122) — while it is on, click any item in the 3D view to put the pivot at that item's centre. The selection does not change.

Quick Move and Step

  1. Move To Click (ALT 130) — press it, then click a point on the model: the selection moves so that the middle of its underside sits on that point. Handy for putting equipment on a floor. It works once; clicking empty space cancels it.
  2. Unit — mm, cm or m for the step.
  3. Step (ALT 190 / ALT 191) — how far the Move buttons go, and how much Bigger / Smaller changes the size. − / + change it by 0.25.

Move, Scale and Rotate

  1. R, L, B, F, T, Bo (ALT 160 … ALT 165) — move the selection one step towards the right, left, back, front, top or bottom, the faces of the view cube. If you rename the cube's faces in Settings, the letters follow.
  2. Bigger (ALT 150) — makes the selection bigger in all directions, so that its longest side grows by one step.
  3. Smaller (ALT 151) — the same, smaller.
  4. T→F and the other turn buttons (ALT 170 … ALT 175) — turn the selection 90° so that the side that was on top faces front (T→F), back, left or right, or so that the front faces right (F→R) or left (F→L).

Bigger, Smaller and the turn buttons work around your pivot if you have locked one, otherwise around the middle of the selection.

Reset and History

  1. Reset Sel (ALT 140) — puts the selected items back where they were in the model file.
  2. Reset All (ALT 141) — puts every moved item back, selected or not.
  3. Undo (ALT 101) — undoes the last move, turn, resize or reset.
  4. Redo (ALT 102) — does it again.

Step by step

Common tasks

Move an item by an exact distance

  1. Select it, here the loudspeaker /SX-HA86-011A.
  2. Set the Step, for example m and 1.00.
  3. Press T under Move to lift it one metre. Press again to go further, or Undo to take a step back.

Drag an item into place

  1. Select it and press Move under On/Off. Arrows appear on the selection.
  2. Drag an arrow. The item follows while you drag and stays where you let go.
  3. To set it down on a floor or a beam instead, press Move To Click and click the spot.

Turn an item

  1. Select it and press Rotate under On/Off.
  2. Drag a ring. Hold Shift to turn in 15° steps. For exact quarter turns, use the buttons under Rotate instead.

Use a pivot

By default an item turns around its own middle. To turn a valve around its flange, or a hatch around its hinge, set a pivot first:

  1. Select the item and press Set Pivot. Dashed arrows and an orange marker appear at the middle of the selection.
  2. Drag the arrows to move the marker to where the item should turn. Or switch on Item Pivot and click another item to put the pivot at its centre.
  3. Press Lock. Rotate, Scale, Bigger, Smaller and the 90° buttons now work around the pivot.
  4. The pivot is dropped when you select something else, or when you press Reset in the Pivot group.

Put everything back

Undo steps back one change at a time. Reset Sel puts the selected items back in their original place in one go, and Reset All does that for the whole model. Resets can be undone too.

Good to know

What is kept, and what is exported

  • Moves only change what you see in TreDeSpace. The model files are not changed.
  • Moves are not kept when you reload the page, and viewpoints do not store them.
  • To keep them, save a state snapshot in the Export panel (open it from the Panel tab of the ribbon or with CTRL+E) with Save transforms ticked. Loading the snapshot later puts the items back where you moved them.
  • Exports from the Export panel (GLB, IFC and TDP) write the items where they are now, moved, turned and resized.
  • Selecting, and measuring afterwards, work on the items where they are now. Labels and measurements you made before the move stay where they were.
