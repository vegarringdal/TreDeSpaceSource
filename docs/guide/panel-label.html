Panel

Label

The Label panel puts text labels on the model. A label is pinned to a point in 3D and follows it when you move around; a thin leader line points from the label to that spot. Place labels one by one, or create a whole batch from a list of tag names. Labels can be styled, spread out so they don't overlap, saved to a file and turned into viewpoints.

Open the panel

On the ribbon, open the Panel tab 1 and click Label 2, or press CTRL+L.

A tour of the panel

  1. New label — the next click in the 3D view places a label there.
  2. Mute all — hides every label from the view without deleting it. Press again (Show all) to bring them back.
  3. Highlight — selects in the model the items that the selected labels (or all labels) belong to.
  4. Select / deselect / invert the labels, or select the ones inside the clipping box.
  5. Explode / Implode — spreads crowded labels out, and puts them back.
  6. Save… / Load… / Undo / Redo.
  7. Style — colours, opacity and the 3D marker sphere.
  8. Import tags — creates labels from a pasted list of names.
  9. The list — one row per label: select, edit the text, move or delete it.

Working with labels in the 3D view

  • Drag a label to move it. The leader line keeps pointing at its spot.
  • Ctrl + click a label to select it, or to unselect it.
  • Alt + click a label, then click in the model, to move the spot it points at.
  • You can have up to 200 labels.

Place a label by hand

  1. Press New label 1. The button stays highlighted while it waits for your click.
  2. Click the spot in the 3D view. A label called Label 1 appears there.
  3. Change the text in the list 2. The other controls on the row: the swatch 1 selects the label, the target 3 moves it (click the button, then the new spot), and the bin 4 deletes it. The grey line below 5 links the label to an item in the model. Labels made from tags fill it in for you.

Label a list of tags

If you have a list of tag numbers, for example from a spreadsheet or a report, the panel can put a label on every one of them at once.

  1. Open the Import tags section and paste the names, one per line 1. They are matched against the item names in the model. The leading / is optional.
  2. Choose the options: 2 Snap anchor to nearest item pins the label to the nearest part of the tag instead of the middle of its bounding box. Use it for long pipes, where the middle can be in empty air. 3 Label text without leading / gives cleaner text. The link to the model is not affected. 4 Store limits the search to one store.
  3. Press Append 5 to add the labels to the ones you already have (tags that already have a label are skipped), or Replace 6 to delete all labels first.
  4. Names that were not found are written back into the box, so you can correct them and try again.

Change how labels look

  1. Select the labels to change: Select all, Ctrl + click in the view, or click their swatches in the list. The top line of Style says what will change 1. With nothing selected, the style applies to the next labels you create.
  2. Pick the Label color 2 and Text color 3. Opacity makes the label background see-through, and Leader line sets the colour of the line.
  3. Turn on 3D sphere at the anchor 4 to mark the exact spot with a small sphere. Unlike the label, the sphere sits in the model, so parts in front of it hide it. Set its size and colour, and make it Solid if a wireframe is hard to see.
  4. Styled text 5 lets labels have several lines and **bold** words.

Spread out crowded labels

  1. Press Explode 1. The labels move outward from the middle of the group so they no longer overlap. Press again to push them further. 3 chooses whether they are laid out on a Circle or a Box.
  2. Implode 2 puts every label back on its spot. This also undoes labels you dragged by hand.

Make one viewpoint per label

Good for walking a list of tags with a colleague: each label becomes a saved view that looks at its item.

  1. Select the labels, for example with Select all 1. Only labels that are linked to an item can be used.
  2. Press Selected to viewpoints 2.
  3. The Viewpoint Viewer opens with one viewpoint per label, named after the label. Click one to fly there. The item is selected and its label is shown. If a viewpoint for the same item and name already exists, it is not created twice.

Save, load and undo

  • Save… downloads all labels and their style as labels.json. Load… replaces the current labels with a saved file.
  • Undo / Redo step through label changes: placing, moving, deleting, importing and styling.
  • Delete all removes every label. You can still undo it.
