Viewpoint

Save views of the model — camera, clipping, labels, measurements, colours and a selection — and step through them in a review or a presentation. Numbered markers on each picture match the numbers in the text; click a picture to see it full size.

App layout · F10

The Viewpoint layout

Press F10 (or pick Viewpoint on the App Layout ribbon) to open the viewpoint workspace. A viewpoint is a saved view with a name. Activating it flies the camera there and brings back everything that belongs to it: clipping, labels, measurements, colours and selected items. Build the viewpoints in the Viewpoint Editor, then present them one by one from the Viewpoint Viewer.

  1. Home ribbon — the same as in F1 Home.
  2. Hierarchy — the model tree. The items a viewpoint selects are highlighted here too.
  3. Viewpoint Viewer — the list for presenting: click a name to go there. See below.
  4. 3D view.
  5. Viewpoint Editor — create, name, describe, order and delete viewpoints. See below.
  6. Measurement (viewpoint) — the measurements of the active viewpoint.
  7. Set Color (viewpoint) — the colour rules of the active viewpoint.
  8. Label (viewpoint) — the labels of the active viewpoint.

Viewpoints are not kept when you reload the page. They belong to the model that is loaded right now, so every session starts with an empty list. Use Save in the Viewpoint Editor to keep them in a file, and Load to bring them back.

Concept

What a viewpoint remembers

When you activate a viewpoint, the viewer…

  • Flies the camera to the saved position, and switches to perspective or orthographic view and to sketch mode or not, as saved.
  • Sets the clipping as saved: the clipping box, the clipping planes and the clip shapes. See F2 Clip Plane and F3 Clip Box.
  • Shows its own labels and measurements instead of the ones you had on screen. Yours are put aside, not deleted (see Scene or viewpoint).
  • Runs its colour rules, if it has any. A viewpoint without rules leaves the colours as they are.
  • Selects its items — the names in its Selected on activation list. If the list is empty, everything is unselected.

The name and description are shown in the Viewpoint Viewer. The badge on each row of the editor counts what is stored: 5L 1M 5S 2C means 5 labels, 1 measurement, 5 selected items and 2 colour rules.

Step by step

Common tasks

Create a viewpoint

  1. Set up the view: move the camera, and turn on a clipping box or planes if you need them. Here we fly to the public-address system /HA-TELE-PA.
  2. In the Viewpoint Editor, press Add viewpoint 1 (ALT 1126). A new viewpoint called Viewpoint 1 (2, 3 …) is added at the end of the list and made active.
  3. The new viewpoint holds only the camera and clipping. It starts with no labels, measurements, colour rules or selection, so the labels and measurements you had on screen disappear when it activates, and the selection is cleared. They are not lost: Unmute scene brings them back (see Scene or viewpoint).
  4. Open its row and type a Name 1 and a Description 2. These are saved as you type.
  5. To fill the viewpoint, either copy what you already have, or edit it directly:
    • Copy labels 3, Copy measurements 4 and Copy set colors 5 copy the labels and measurements you made outside the viewpoint, and the rules of the normal Set Color panel, into this viewpoint. Each one replaces what the viewpoint had.
    • Or use the three (viewpoint) panels on the right — see Change a viewpoint's labels, measurements or colours.
  6. To have items selected when the viewpoint opens, select them (in the Hierarchy or the 3D view) and press From selection 7, or type the names into the list 6, one per line.

Many viewpoints at once: the Label panel can turn every selected label into its own viewpoint that looks at the labelled item. See Make one viewpoint per label.

Change a viewpoint's labels, measurements or colours

The three (viewpoint) panels always work on the active viewpoint. They are locked until you press Edit, and nothing you change sticks until you press Save to viewpoint.

  1. Activate the viewpoint (Activate in the editor, or click its name in the Viewpoint Viewer).
  2. The panels show Viewing and the viewpoint's name 1 and are greyed out. Press Edit 2 (ALT 1185). This unlocks all three panels.
  3. Make your changes. Use the panels as you normally would: place or import labels, change colour rules. Labels you place and measurements you take in the 3D view while the viewpoint is active also belong to the viewpoint.
  4. As soon as something differs from what is saved, the panels show unsaved edits 1 and Save to viewpoint 2 lights up. Press it (ALT 1138) to store the labels, measurements and colour rules in the viewpoint. One press saves all three panels.
  5. If you activate another viewpoint, or press Unmute scene, while there are unsaved edits, the viewer asks first. Save 1 stores them; Discard 2 throws them away and the viewpoint stays as it was saved.

Update the camera or clipping of a viewpoint

  1. Move the camera and change the clipping as you want it.
  2. Open the viewpoint's row in the editor and press Update camera/clip 6 (ALT 1128). The camera position and all clipping (box, planes and shapes) are replaced with what you have now. Labels, measurements, colours and selection are not touched.

Order and delete viewpoints

  • The arrows 3 4 in each row header (picture above) move the viewpoint up or down. The order is the order of the Viewpoint Viewer.
  • 2 inserts a new viewpoint (current camera and clipping, otherwise empty) above this one. Unlike Add viewpoint, it does not activate it.
  • Delete 7 removes one viewpoint straight away, without asking, and it cannot be undone. If it was active, your own labels and measurements come back.
  • Delete all in the top row removes every viewpoint, after asking.

Present viewpoints

  1. Load or create the viewpoints. If you got them as a file, press Load in the Viewpoint Viewer.
  2. Click a name in the Viewpoint Viewer. The camera flies there and the viewpoint's labels, measurements, colours and selection appear. Its description is shown under the name.
  3. Use Mute labels or Mute measures to take them off the screen for a moment, without deleting anything. See The Viewpoint Viewer.

Save viewpoints to a file and load them again

  • Save (ALT 1130) in the Viewpoint Editor downloads every viewpoint, with all its content, as viewpoints.json.
  • Load — in the editor or in the Viewpoint Viewer — reads such a file. It replaces all the viewpoints you have, without asking. No viewpoint is active after loading; click one to start.
  • The file only makes sense with the same model loaded: items are found by name, and positions are in model coordinates.

Panel

The Viewpoint Editor

The editor lists every viewpoint as a row you can open. The active one is shown in blue with — active after its name. Only one row is open at a time; opening a row does not activate it.

  1. Add viewpoint (ALT 1126) — a new viewpoint from the current camera and clipping, made active straight away.
  2. Save (ALT 1130) — downloads all viewpoints as a file.
  3. Load — replaces all viewpoints with the ones in a file.
  4. Delete all (ALT 1228) — removes every viewpoint, after asking.
  5. A viewpoint — click the name to open its row.
  6. The active viewpoint.

A host application that embeds the viewer can add one more button to this row, for example to store the viewpoints as a bookmark in that application.

A viewpoint row

  1. Badge — how many labels (L), measurements (M), selected items (S) and colour rules (C) the viewpoint holds.
  2. Insert before — a new, empty viewpoint above this one.
  3. Move up.
  4. Move down.
  5. Activate (ALT 1127) — go to the viewpoint. See What a viewpoint remembers.
  6. Update camera/clip (ALT 1128) — store the current camera and clipping in this viewpoint.
  7. Delete (ALT 1129) — remove this viewpoint. No undo.
  1. Name.
  2. Description — shown under the name in the Viewpoint Viewer when the viewpoint is active. Start a new line with Enter; put ** around words to make them bold.
  3. Copy labels (ALT 1132) — replaces the viewpoint's labels with the ones you made outside the viewpoint.
  4. Copy measurements (ALT 1133) — the same for measurements.
  5. Copy set colors (ALT 1134) — replaces the viewpoint's colour rules with the rules in the normal Set Color panel. If that panel was never used, the viewpoint ends up with no rules.
  6. Selected on activation — item names, one per line, selected when the viewpoint activates.
  7. From selection (ALT 1135) — fills the list from what is selected now.

Name, description, the selection list, the Copy buttons and Update camera/clip change the viewpoint immediately — no Save to viewpoint needed. Only the three (viewpoint) panels wait for Save to viewpoint.

Panel

The Viewpoint Viewer

A plain list for presenting: no editing, just a click per viewpoint. Open it on its own from the Panel tab of the ribbon, or with ALT 1125.

  1. Load — replaces all viewpoints with the ones in a file.
  2. Mute labels (ALT 1139) — hides every label from the 3D view; the button then reads Labels off. Press again to show them. The labels are not deleted, and the setting stays when you switch viewpoints.
  3. Mute measures (ALT 720) — the same for measurements (Measures off).
  4. The active viewpoint — click any name to activate it.
  5. Description of the active viewpoint.

Panels

The (viewpoint) panels

Label (viewpoint), Measurement (viewpoint) and Set Color (viewpoint) are the normal Label, Measurement List and Set Color panels, but they work on the active viewpoint's own labels, measurements and colour rules. How the panels themselves work is explained on their own pages:

  • Label guide →
  • Measurement List guide →
  • Set Color guide →

What is different:

  • With no active viewpoint, they only show No active viewpoint — activate one in the Viewpoints panel first.
  • They are locked until you press Edit, and changes only stick with Save to viewpoint — see Change a viewpoint's labels, measurements or colours. Edit and Save to viewpoint act on all three panels together.

Set Color (viewpoint)

The top line 1 names the viewpoint whose rules you see. They run every time the viewpoint is activated, in the run mode chosen here: with Reset model, activating the viewpoint first removes all other colours. Edit 2 unlocks the panel; Run 3 tries the rules right away. Save / Load in this panel save the rule set to a file or load one into the viewpoint (press Save to viewpoint afterwards).

Set Color is not muted like labels and measurements: the normal Set Color panel keeps its own rules and works at the same time.

Scene or viewpoint: who owns the labels

Your own labels and measurements — the ones in the normal Label and Measurement List panels — are the scene. A viewpoint has its own. Only one side is on screen at a time:

  1. When a viewpoint is activated, its labels and measurements are shown, and the normal Label and Measurement List panels are muted: they only show this message. Your scene labels are kept aside. Press Unmute scene 1 (ALT 1136) to get them back.
  2. Now the (viewpoint) panels are muted instead. The camera, clipping and colours do not change. Press Unmute viewpoint 1 (ALT 1137) to show the viewpoint's labels and measurements again, without moving the camera.

Deleting the active viewpoint, Delete all and Load also bring your scene labels and measurements back.
