App layout · F8

The Panels layout

Press F8 (or pick Panels on the App Layout ribbon) when you want to put your own workspace together. The Panel ribbon lists every panel in the app, and Settings opens on its Layouts tab, where the layout slots are named.

  1. Panel ribbon — one button per panel. See below.
  2. Hierarchy — the model tree, described in F1 Home.
  3. 3D view.
  4. Settings → Layouts — rename the 24 layout slots, or reset them. See Rename or reset a layout.
  5. Console — folded. Click its tab to open it.

Ribbon

The Panel ribbon

  1. A panel that is open is highlighted (here Hierarchy, Settings and Console). Click it to close the panel.
  2. A panel that is closed is not highlighted. Click it to open the panel on its usual side of the screen, or drag the button to exactly where you want the panel (see Move a panel).
  3. External Panels — your organisation's own tools that open in a panel (set up under Settings → External, see F7 External). This group is only shown when there are such tools; here it holds an example tool, Work Orders. One added during this session appears here once it has been opened.

The highlight always follows the screen, however a panel was opened or closed. A panel you close and open again comes back where it was. Most panels also have a shortcut to show or hide them: the reference table lists them all (for example CTRL+C for Set Color, CTRL+S for Settings). The 3D view has no button: it is always there.

Arranging the screen

Moving panels around

The panel header

Every group of panels has a header with a tab per panel. Here Set Color was opened next to Settings, so they share the right side as two tabs.

  1. Tab — click it to show that panel. Drag it to move the panel. Double-click it to float the panel in its own window.
  2. × — closes the panel. Open it again from the Panel ribbon.
  3. Size lock (padlock) — click to lock the group at its current size: it can still grow, but it is never squeezed smaller when you drag other dividers. A closed padlock means locked; click again to unlock.
  4. ▾ Fold — folds the whole group down to its header, to give the space to its neighbours. It changes to ▸; click that to unfold. Panels keep their contents while folded.
  • To resize two panels, drag the thin line between them.
  • The ribbon at the top has a ▾ at its right end too: it folds the ribbon away for more room. The ribbon itself cannot be moved or closed.

Move a panel

  1. Press on a panel's tab (or its button on the Panel ribbon) and drag. A label with the panel's name follows the pointer.
  2. Move over the panel you want to put it next to. A set of drop buttons appears in the middle of that panel.
    • 1 Centre — add it as a tab in that panel's group.
    • 2 Inner ring (left, right, top, bottom) — split that panel and put the new one on that side of it.
    • 3 Outer ring (smaller buttons) — put it beside the whole column or row the panel sits in, rather than inside it.
  3. Release the mouse button on a drop button. The blue area shows the result before you let go. Releasing on another panel's tab strip adds it as a tab at that spot.
  4. Released anywhere else, the panel floats in its own window. Press Esc while dragging to cancel.

Floating windows

  1. Title bar — drag it to move the window. Double-click it to dock the panel back. Drag the window's edges or corners to resize it.
  2. – — folds the window down to its title bar. Click again to restore it.
  3. ⤓ Dock back — puts the panel back into the main screen.
  4. × — closes the window and its panel.

A floating window can hold several panels as tabs: drag another panel's tab onto its tab strip.

More room for the 3D view

  • Solo main panel (ALT 626) — closes every panel except the 3D view. Press again to bring them all back as they were.
  • Kiosk mode (ALT 1175) — the 3D view only: panels closed and the ribbon folded away. Press again to return.

Good to know

Keeping your arrangement

The panel arrangement itself is not remembered when you reload the page: the app starts in the layout slot you last chose (for example F8), as that slot was saved. To keep an arrangement, save it into a layout slot: Save your own layout. Its name is changed under Settings → Layouts, on the right of this layout (Rename or reset a layout).
