Settings

How the 3D view looks, how the camera moves, your keyboard shortcuts and more. Numbered markers on each picture match the numbers in the text; click a picture to see it full size.

On this page

Panel

The Settings panel

Settings is open on the right in F1 Home, and in F8 Panels on its Layouts tab. Show or hide it anywhere with CTRL+S, or from the Panel ribbon. Every change takes effect at once and is remembered in your browser; there is no OK button.

  1. Tabs — click one to see its settings.
  2. Section — click the title to fold or unfold it.
  3. Info (ⓘ) — explains what the section is for.
  4. Section reset — puts just that section back to its defaults. It is greyed out while the section is already at its defaults, so it also tells you at a glance which sections you have changed.
  5. Save… — downloads your settings as tredespace-settings.json.
  6. Load… — reads such a file back, for example on another computer.
  7. Reset all settings — puts everything back to the defaults, after asking first.

What Save, Load and Reset all cover

  • Rendering, Lighting, GPU, Navigation, Edges, Ambient Occlusion, Gizmo, Stats and Editor, the light / dark theme, and your own keyboard shortcuts. Save and Load also include the colour picker swatches.
  • Not the layout slots and not the external apps: those are reset from their own tabs (Layouts and External).
  • The camera type (Persp. / Ortho) and Sketch mode are not settings: the app always starts in perspective with Sketch off.
  • Settings live in this browser only. Wipe All on the Home ribbon erases them; save a file first if you want to keep them.

Tab by tab

The tabs

Most settings have a keyboard shortcut; the reference tables at the end list every one. Settings marked advanced are for troubleshooting and seldom need changing.

Rendering

Picture quality and the selection look. The tab has nine sections; the ones that matter most:

  • Antialiasing — smooths jagged edges. MSAA 4x (on by default) smooths every frame. AA fast instead builds up a smooth picture over several frames while the camera stands still; AA samples is how many. Smart pixel ratio picks a sharp render resolution for your screen; untick it to set the Pixel ratio yourself (lower is faster but blurrier).
  • Transparency — how see-through items are drawn. Blend (default) draws them as glass. Alpha hash uses a fine dot pattern that smooths out when the camera stops, and avoids glass drawn in the wrong order. Background draws the see-through items solid, behind everything else, so the items you left solid always stand in front ("ghosted context"); Background fade sets how far their colours fade towards the background (0 % keeps their colours, 100 % gives flat silhouettes). Items in the background cannot be clicked.
  • Background & selection — the colour of the 3D view behind the model, the colour of the selection tint, and whether a selection shows as a tint, an outline or both (the same choice as on the Home ribbon).
  • Outline (selection & hover) — strength, glow, thickness, pulse and colours of the outline. Hover outline also outlines the item under the mouse.
  • Picking — Pick opacity ≥ (default 10.1 %): items at least this solid can be clicked. Fainter items let the click pass through to what is behind them. Hold Shift while clicking to do the opposite: pick faint items and click through glass.
  • Dark colours — models with pure black colours show no shading at all. Lift black color to grey (on by default) draws pure black as grey, at the Grey level you choose. Only exact black is changed, only on screen.
  • VRAM budget — for very large models or graphics cards with little memory. Off by default: everything loads in full detail. When Enabled, models far from the camera switch to a coarse version while the camera stands still, to stay under Max VRAM, and come back in full detail when you move close and stop. Set it too low and parts you are looking at stay coarse or are missing. Leave it off unless you need it. Show activity indicator puts a small sign in the corner of the 3D view while it works.
  • Culling and Debug — advanced. FPS limit (default 30) caps how often the picture is redrawn; the rest tunes how the renderer skips tiny or hidden parts, or shows its internal buffers.

Lighting

Two lights shade the model: an ambient light that lights every surface evenly, and a headlight that follows the camera and gives shape to what you look at. Set the colour and intensity of each (0 turns a light off; above 1 overexposes). Sketch lighting is a separate set used only while Sketch mode is on.

GPU

Advanced. On a computer with two graphics cards, choose which one the app asks for. Next to each choice is the card it gives on this computer (the picture was taken on a machine without a real graphics card). A new choice needs Reload to apply. Simulate GPU crash is a test: it makes the app lose its graphics card on purpose, to check that it recovers.

Navigation

  • Mode — Orbit turns and pans around a centre point; pressing a movement key (W A S D, E Q) switches to fly or walk. Fly (default) moves freely in the direction you look. Walk moves at a constant height, like walking through the plant. TAB switches between fly and walk.
  • Walk when movement keys are used — the movement keys go to walk instead of fly when you leave orbit.
  • Fly / Walk speed in metres per second, and the faster + Shift speeds used while you hold Shift.
  • Orbit and Pan sensitivity — how much the view turns or slides per mouse movement or arrow key press.
  • Frame dense bounds on load — when a model loads, frame the area where most of it is, so a few parts far away don't make the first view tiny.

Edges

  • Geometry edges — lines where a surface bends or one part is in front of another. Item edges — lines along the borders between items. Both are on by default. Item edges can be switched off for single items from the Hierarchy right-click menu.
  • Edge colour, and White edges on dark items so lines stay visible on dark surfaces (Darkness threshold decides what counts as dark).
  • Edges — flat shading and Edges — with normals — fine-tuning for the two kinds of model surfaces: Fade makes far-away lines fainter; the two thresholds decide how sharp a bend or depth jump must be to get a line. Lower values give more lines.
  • Sketch edges — the line settings, colour mode and view cube colours used while Sketch is on.

Ambient Occlusion

Ambient occlusion darkens corners, creases and places where parts touch, so the model looks more solid. It is Off by default. Motion computes it in every frame (costs speed while you move); Static builds a cleaner result while the camera stands still. Radius is how far the shadowing reaches, Strength how dark it gets; Slices and Samples trade quality against speed.

Gizmo

The names on the faces of the view cube in the corner of the 3D view (FRONT, BACK, LEFT, RIGHT, TOP, BOT), for example to use your plant's own directions. The coloured face buttons on the ribbons show their first letters. Cube colours sets the cube's faces, lines, text and hover colour.

Shortcuts

  1. Import / export — Export… saves the shortcuts you have changed to a file; Import… applies such a file (shortcuts that clash with others are skipped). The reset button in its header removes all your changes.
  2. Search — find a shortcut by name, description or key combination (for example ALT 6). Below it, the shortcuts are grouped by category; the badge shows how many there are, or how many you have changed.
  3. Camera / navigation — the mouse and movement keys of the 3D view. They are fixed and cannot be changed.

To change a shortcut:

  1. Search for it, or open its category.
  2. Press Record 3. The key field turns blue and reads press keys… 1.
  3. Press the new keys. Keys pressed together become one step, shown joined with + (like CTRL+S); release them and press more keys to add another step, shown after a space (like ALT 1010).
  4. Stop for a moment, or press Enter, and the new shortcut is stored. Esc cancels.
  5. If the keys are already used by another shortcut, the app asks whether to give them to this one instead.

A changed shortcut is shown in orange. The × next to it 2 puts it back to the default; it is greyed out while the shortcut is unchanged.

Stats

Advanced. Live numbers from the renderer: models, triangles, memory use, frame times. Useful when the view is slow or when you report a problem. Show overlay in viewport shows the list in the corner of the 3D view; untick rows to leave them out of the overlay.

Editor

The grid of colours at the bottom of every colour picker in the app: click a swatch to change it, or Reset swatches. Use dark theme is the same switch as Dark on the Home ribbon (ALT 401).

External

Usually set up once by your administrator: the web tools that open from the External ribbon, and which other web pages may control the viewer (API security). Reset external apps removes them all; Reset all settings does not touch them. See F7 External.

Layouts

The names of the 24 layout slots (F1–F12 and ALT+F1–ALT+F12), with a reset button per slot and Reset layouts at the bottom. Explained in Rename or reset a layout.

About

The app version (give it when you report a problem), links to the product page and the source code, the licence, and the notices for the third-party software the app uses. All processing happens in your browser: your models never leave your computer.

Reference

Every control, tab by tab

Number fields have a − and a + button, each with its own shortcut. Colour fields and text fields have no shortcut and are mostly left out. The Shortcuts tab is its own reference.
